Special Baggage & Equipment Guidelines

Rules for infants, musical instruments, golf sets, pets, and mobility gear.

Infant & Child Allowance

Infants without seat usually get 1 PC 10 KG checked bag + 1 fully collapsible stroller / pram + car seat checked free of charge.

Golf & Sports Equipment

Gulf carriers & Qatar Airways allow 1 Golf bag free in lieu of standard checked allowance. Scuba, skis, and bikes require prior notice.

Musical Instruments

Small instruments (violins, flutes) count as hand baggage if within 115cm dimensions. Large instruments (cellos) require purchasing an extra seat.

Wheelchairs & Mobility Aids

Wheelchairs and assistance devices are checked FREE of charge across all airlines and do not count towards standard baggage quotas.

Pets (In-Cabin & Cargo)

Pets under 8kg (including carrier) permitted in cabin on select carriers (Lufthansa, Air France). Others require cargo hold transport with health certificates.
