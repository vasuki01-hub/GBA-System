ICAO & IATA Prohibited / Restricted Baggage Registry

Universal security guidelines for carry-on and checked luggage restrictions.

Lithium-Ion Batteries & Power Banks

Must be carried strictly in Cabin Baggage only. Checked luggage carriage of spare lithium batteries or power banks is strictly prohibited under international aviation fire safety rules.

Liquids, Aerosols, and Gels (LAGs)

In carry-on baggage, liquids must be in containers of maximum 100ml packed inside a single transparent, resealable 1-litre plastic bag.

Flammable Substances & Fireworks

Strictly prohibited in both carry-on and checked baggage, including camping stoves, lighter refills, paint thinner, and sparklers.
